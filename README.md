Hi! I'm Vitumbiko aka Vitu. I am an Industrial Engineer with a strong passion for data, analytics, and process optimization, currently working at **Fast n Fresh** within supply chain and warehouse operations.

In my current role, I focus on improving operational efficiency through structured problem-solving, process analysis, and data-driven decision-making. I develop and refine Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), analyse operational performance data, improve reporting accuracy, and support continuous improvement initiatives across logistics and inventory processes.

Beyond operations, I have built a strong foundation in Data Engineering and Business Intelligence. I have hands-on experience designing modern data warehouse architectures using a Bronze–Silver–Gold layered approach, implementing dimensional modeling and star schema designs to create business-ready analytics models. I enjoy transforming raw, unstructured data into clean, structured insights that support strategic and operational decisions.

My technical interests include:

* SQL & Data Modeling
* Data Warehousing Architecture
* Business Intelligence & Dashboarding (Power BI)
* Process Optimization & Continuous Improvement
* Supply Chain Analytics

I am particularly interested in opportunities within banking, logistics, retail, and data-driven organisations where I can bridge operations and analytics — turning complex business processes into measurable, optimised, and insight-driven systems.

I am continuously learning, building, and refining my skills in Data Engineering and BI to become a well-rounded analytics professional who drives real business impact.
